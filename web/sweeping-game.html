Your moves

    How to play

    1. You start at the broom place with the broom lifted.
    2. Click a corner, a junction or a line end to carry the broom there in a straight line.
    3. Click a line piece that starts where you stand to sweep it, or click the point at its other end. A run goes on as long as you keep sweeping.
    4. To stop at a corner or junction, lift the broom: click your own point, press Lift broom or L. The next point you click is then reached by carrying, even when a dusty line leads there.
    5. Every line piece is swept once. When all are clean, click the broom square to put the broom back.

    Speed is 1 m/s for sweeping and for carrying, so seconds equal metres walked. The court has the ITF dimensions: 10.97 m doubles width, 8.23 m singles width, service line 6.40 m from the net. The 10 cm centre mark is swept in passing with the baseline, as on a real court. The lines total 73.14 m. The solver's route is provably optimal, so the best you can do is a tie.
